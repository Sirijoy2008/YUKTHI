# Project Overview: FINOVA

## Problem Statement
Indian consumers frequently encounter suspicious payment requests, experience failed or delayed UPI transactions, and struggle to forecast their short-term cash flow when funds are locked in uncertain states.

## Context and Evidence
With the rapid adoption of digital payments in India, the volume of social engineering scams and transaction disputes has risen. Users often lack a centralized tool that guides them through both risk assessment and the practical next steps for resolving payment issues, while simultaneously showing the impact on their personal cash flow.

## Target Users and Personas
- **Target Market:** India
- **Persona 1:** The careful digital novice who needs reassurance and clear steps when receiving a suspicious SMS about a "blocked bank account."
- **Persona 2:** The gig worker or freelancer who relies on timely payments and needs to project cash flow when a client payment is delayed.

## User Pain Points
- Uncertainty about whether a message or payment link is a scam.
- Confusion over what to do when money is debited but not credited.
- Difficulty managing upcoming expenses when expected income is delayed.

## Proposed Solution
FINOVA provides a unified action centre combining a Scam Checker, Payment Assistant, and Cash-Flow Planner to guide users through financial uncertainties.

## Core Value Proposition
A single platform to assess risk, follow up on payments, and plan cash flow based on the real-world status of those payments.

## Proposed Differentiator
**FINOVA connects financial risk awareness, payment follow-up, and cash-flow planning through a shared financial activity record and a unified action centre.** 
Instead of isolated tools for fraud checking or budgeting, FINOVA links a delayed payment directly to the user's cash-flow forecast and tracks follow-up actions centrally. *(Note: This differentiator requires further market validation through competitor research).*

## Scope
- **In-Scope:** Rule-based text analysis for risk, static guidance for payment follow-ups, manual entry cash-flow planning, hypothetical scenarios.
- **Out-Scope:** Real-time bank integration, actual transaction reversal, live threat intelligence, automated fraud blocking.

## Assumptions Requiring Validation
- Users are willing to manually input their financial statuses and upcoming expenses.
- Rule-based analysis will provide sufficient value without producing dangerous false negatives.

## Measurable Success Criteria
- Successful completion of the 3 core workflows during testing.
- Clear distinction in the UI between confirmed and unconfirmed funds.
- Zero requests for sensitive user credentials (e.g., OTP, UPI PIN) across the application.
