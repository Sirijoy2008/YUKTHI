# Competitor Research

> **Note:** The following represents theoretical research on existing solutions to validate FINOVA's proposed differentiator. No primary field research or user interviews have been conducted.

## 1. Truecaller / SMS Filtering Apps
- **Target Users:** General mobile users.
- **Main Capabilities:** "Smart SMS" functionality identifies smishing. Red alert notifications for potential fraud, automatic link blocking, and restricted message access unless marked safe. Uses community feedback and on-device ML.
- **Evidence Source:** Truecaller Official Documentation (truecaller.com), accessed Oct 9, 2026.
- **Limitations:** Focuses on sender reputation rather than contextual analysis of the message content itself. Does not guide the user on financial next steps if money was already transferred.
- **Overlap with FINOVA:** Scam awareness.
- **Differentiation Opportunity:** FINOVA focuses on contextual rule-based analysis and links the threat directly to financial action.

## 2. Bank Apps & UPI Apps (e.g., Google Pay, PhonePe, SBI YONO)
- **Target Users:** Bank customers and UPI users.
- **Main Capabilities:** Display transaction status (Success, Failed, Pending). Offer dispute resolution tickets.
- **Evidence Source:** National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) guidelines and general app functionality.
- **Limitations:** Isolated to transactions made on their specific platform. They do not help forecast how a delayed payment affects upcoming rent or bills.
- **Overlap with FINOVA:** Payment tracking and dispute logging.
- **Differentiation Opportunity:** FINOVA aggregates the impact of delayed payments across different sources to show the holistic effect on the user's cash flow.

## 3. Expense Trackers (e.g., Walnut/Axio, YNAB)
- **Target Users:** Budget-conscious individuals.
- **Main Capabilities:** Track daily expenses, read SMS to auto-categorize spending, set budgets.
- **Evidence Source:** General market knowledge.
- **Limitations:** They usually treat income as binary (you have it or you don't). They do not explicitly handle "unconfirmed" or "stuck" UPI payments as a specific workflow for budgeting.
- **Overlap with FINOVA:** Cash-flow planning.
- **Differentiation Opportunity:** FINOVA explicitly handles the uncertainty of digital payments and allows users to model "what-if" scenarios based on pending transactions.

## Summary of Findings
While there are robust tools for spam blocking, payment processing, and budgeting, they exist in silos. FINOVA's differentiator—**connecting a delayed payment to cash-flow forecasting and unified action tracking**—appears distinct in the current market, as most tools do not cross the boundary between payment dispute and personal budgeting.
